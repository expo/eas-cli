Add `eas integrations:surrealdb:connect`, `eas integrations:surrealdb:dashboard`, and `eas integrations:surrealdb:disconnect` to connect an app to a [SurrealDB](https://surrealdb.com) instance: install the SDK and write `EXPO_PUBLIC_SURREAL_ENDPOINT`, `EXPO_PUBLIC_SURREAL_NAMESPACE`, and `EXPO_PUBLIC_SURREAL_DATABASE` to `.env.local` and EAS.
